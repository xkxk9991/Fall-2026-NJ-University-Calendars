One-time setup needed Edit the BASE constant at the bottom of this file to your GitHub Pages URL, then the buttons below will work.

How to subscribe

  1. Click Subscribe — it opens Google Calendar with the feed pre-filled.
  2. Confirm, then rename the calendar (three dots → Settings) since Google names it after the URL.
  3. Repeat for each school. Each gets its own colour and can be toggled independently.
Google refreshes external calendars slowly Typically once every 24 hours, sometimes less. Updates published Sunday morning may not appear until Monday or Tuesday. Subscribed calendars are also read-only — copy an event to your own calendar if you need to annotate it.
Compiled from official university sources. Every event carries its source URL in the description. Where a school hasn't published a schedule, there's no event rather than a guess.
